DualCam
-------

Capture with Two Cameras at Once
Product Requirements Document (PRD)

1. Product Overview
DualCam is an Android camera app that captures video or photos from two cameras simultaneously (e.g., front + rear), with customizable templates, ratios, and output options (combined or separate raw files).

2. Goals
- Enable simultaneous dual-camera capture.
- Provide flexible layout templates and aspect ratios.
- Allow users to save a combined output and/or separate raw files for editing.
- Offer a simple, high-quality, and customizable experience.

3. Key Features
- Dual Camera Capture – Record or take photos from two cameras at the same time.
- Templates – Choose from various split-screen layouts (e.g., Side by Side, Picture-in-Picture, Top-Bottom, etc.).
- Aspect Ratios – Set overall output ratio (e.g., 16:9, 9:16, 1:1) and individual ratios for each camera.
- Output Options – Save combined media, separate raw front and rear files, or both.
- Live Preview – See the template and both camera feeds in real time.
- Camera Controls – Switch cameras, tap to focus, exposure, flash, and more.
- Gallery & Management – View, play, share, or delete captured media.
- Settings – Resolution, bitrate/quality, storage, and more.

4. Supported Templates (Examples)
    1. Side by Side
    2. Top & Bottom
    3. Picture in Picture
    4. Corner PIP
    5. Diagonal Split
    6. Triple (2+1)
    7. Grid (2×2)
    8. Custom

5. User Flow

    1. Open DualCam.
    2. Choose mode (Video / Photo).
    3. Select a template.
    4. Set aspect ratios & output options.
    5. Capture (record or take photo).
    6. View, manage, and share from gallery.

6. Tech Stack
- Language: Kotlin
- Camera: CameraX
- Media Processing: Media3 (merge, encode, mux)
- UI: Jetpack Compose (or XML)
- Storage: Scoped Storage (MediaStore)
- Build: Gradle (Android Studio)
- Target: Android 8.0+ (API 26+)

7. Functional Requirements (FR)
- FR-01: Capture photo and video from two cameras simultaneously.
- FR-02: Support multiple predefined templates.
- FR-03: Allow custom aspect ratio for overall output and each camera.
- FR-04: Save combined, separate, or both outputs.
- FR-05: Provide real-time preview with the selected template.
- FR-06: Manage captured media (view, share, delete, rename).

8. Non-Functional Requirements (NFR)
- NFR-01: Stable recording (minimal frame drops).
- NFR-02: High-quality output (configurable resolution and bitrate).
- NFR-03: Optimized performance and battery usage.
- NFR-04: Clear and intuitive UI / UX.
- NFR-05: Support a wide range of Android devices (dual/multi-camera capable).

9. MVP Scope (v1.0)
- Dual photo and video capture.
- Predefined templates (Side by Side, Top-Bottom, PIP, etc.).
- Aspect ratio selection.
- Output options (combined / separate / both).
- Basic gallery and sharing.

10. Future Enhancements
- Custom template editor.
- More layout styles (N-cam, collage, etc.).
- Manual camera controls (ISO, shutter, WB, etc.).
- Background recording (video).
- Cloud backup and project export for editing apps.
